PATIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding heart check-ups and cardiology visits

What should I prepare before an ECG or Echocardiogram? ▼
Wear comfortable two-piece clothing. Avoid caffeine, heavy meals, and vigorous exercise 2 hours before your stress test or echocardiography session.
Can I schedule an appointment online with a cardiologist? ▼
Yes. Use our online booking portal to select your preferred consultation date and time slot with our cardiology department.
What emergency cardiac facilities does City Care Hospital provide? ▼
Our 24/7 cardiac emergency unit has round-the-clock cardiac monitoring, rapid response resuscitation, and immediate catheterization capabilities.
