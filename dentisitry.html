PATIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about dental checkups and treatments

How frequently should I schedule a dental cleaning? ▼
Dentists generally recommend a professional dental checkup and ultrasonic cleaning once every six months to prevent calculus buildup and early gum inflammation.
Is root canal therapy painful? ▼
Modern root canal procedures are performed under effective local anesthesia and are virtually painless, relieving the acute toothache caused by pulp infection.
