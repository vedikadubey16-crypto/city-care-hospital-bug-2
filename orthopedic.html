PATIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding orthopedic consultations and recovery

When should I consult an orthopedic specialist for joint pain? ▼
You should seek specialist evaluation if joint pain persists for more than 48 hours, is accompanied by swelling, redness, locking, or restricts your ability to bear weight.
Do all ligament tears require surgery? ▼
Not all tears require surgical intervention. Partial tears and lower-demand injuries often respond well to conservative physical rehabilitation and regenerative therapies.
