What age group is treated in the Pediatrics Department? ▼
Our department provides specialized care for newborns, infants, children, and teenagers up to 18 years of age.
Can I consult about my child's growth and immunization record? ▼
Yes. Please bring your child's immunization card or previous records so our pediatricians can review their developmental timeline and administer any catch-up doses.
