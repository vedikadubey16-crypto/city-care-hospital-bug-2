PATIENT FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful advice on pulmonary health and diagnostic tests

What is a Spirometry lung function test? ▼
Spirometry is a non-invasive breathing test that measures how much air you can breathe in and out, as well as how quickly you can blow the air out. It is used to diagnose asthma, COPD, and other lung conditions.
When should I see a pulmonologist for a chronic cough? ▼
If your cough lasts longer than 3 weeks, causes shortness of breath, wheezing, or chest tightness, you should consult our pulmonology specialists for a clinical evaluation.
